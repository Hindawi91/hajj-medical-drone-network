# Data licensing and attribution

This repository contains two categories of material under different terms.

## 1. Code — MIT License

Everything in `src/` and `notebooks/` is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

## 2. Data products derived from OpenStreetMap — ODbL 1.0

The contents of `data/raw/`, `data/processed/` and `data/model/` are derived from
OpenStreetMap.

> © OpenStreetMap contributors

OpenStreetMap data is open data, licensed under the
[Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/)
by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. See <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.

### What this means if you reuse these files

- **Attribution.** Credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" and make clear the data is
  available under the ODbL.
- **Share-alike.** If you alter or build upon these data and distribute the result **as
  data**, you must release it under the ODbL as well.
- **Figures.** Any figure rendered from OSM-derived data should carry a visible
  "© OpenStreetMap contributors" credit in or beside the figure, not only in a
  bibliography.

The share-alike obligation attaches to the *data*, not to analysis results or prose
describing them.

## 3. Third-party material referenced but not redistributed

- **Basemap tiles** fetched at figure-generation time come from Esri and are subject to
  Esri's terms. No tiles are redistributed in this repository.
- **Manufacturer specifications** (DJI Matrice 350 RTK) are cited in the paper only. No
  manufacturer material is redistributed here.

## 4. Analytical Hajj zones

The four zones used throughout are **analytical circles** centred on known locations with
author-chosen radii (Haram/Central Makkah 4.0 km, Mina 3.0 km, Muzdalifah 2.5 km, Arafat
3.5 km). They are not official administrative boundaries and carry no authority.
